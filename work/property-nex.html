Real-estate data platform · proof of concept

Property Nex

A proof of concept for a platform that turns scattered property data into data products that commercial companies can license.

Open the clickable POC ↗

The problem

Real-estate data sits with many different property owners, developers, brokers, agencies and facility managers, each holding it in its own format. It is hard to analyse and cannot be sold as a product, even though banks, insurers, retailers, logistics firms and investors need exactly this data to decide where to lend, insure, open and invest. Property Nex closes that gap: find the property clients who hold the data, bring it into one consistent model, and license it through a marketplace, with the property clients earning a share of every licence.

What the proof of concept covers
  1. Data sources: property clients analysed and scored on volume, coverage, freshness and exclusivity, so only qualified partners become sources.
  2. Data Studio: every source runs through one pipeline (ingest, clean, de-duplicate, normalise, enrich) into a canonical property data model, with personal data removed.
  3. Dataset module: processed records packaged into licensable products such as a rental index, transaction history, commercial occupancy and industrial lease rates.
  4. Marketplace: buyers preview a dataset and choose a snapshot, subscription or enterprise API licence.
  5. Partner payouts: revenue attributed back to the partners whose records built each licensed dataset, paid as a revenue share.
