Real-estate data platform · confidential

Property Nex

A platform that turns fragmented property-client data into a data product that commercial companies can buy.

The problem

Real-estate data is spread across many property clients in inconsistent formats, so it can’t be analysed or sold as a product, even though commercial companies need it. Property Nex finds and analyses the property clients, processes their data into a consistent model, and puts it on a platform through which the data is sold to commercial companies. Client and commercial details are confidential, so this page shows the approach only.

Approach
  1. Find and analyse the property clients: identify who holds the data and what it is worth.
  2. Collect and process the data: bring each client’s data together, clean it and normalise it into one model.
  3. Build the data module that structures and packages the data.
  4. Build the platform through which the real-estate data is sold to commercial companies.
